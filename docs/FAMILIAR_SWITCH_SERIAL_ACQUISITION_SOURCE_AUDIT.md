# Serial acquisition: physical conventions and statistical sources

[Serial acquisition proof](FAMILIAR_SWITCH_SERIAL_ACQUISITION.md) · [Registered test](FAMILIAR_SWITCH_PROFILED_ACQUISITION.md) · [Earlier realization audit](FAMILIAR_SWITCH_REALIZATION_SOURCE_AUDIT.md) · [Community model](FAMILIAR_SWITCH_COMMUNITY_MODEL.md) · [Earlier serial-reset audit](FAMILIAR_SWITCH_SERIAL_RESET_SOURCE_AUDIT.md)

**Source audit, 28 September 2026.**

**Recommended anchor: mesoscopic single-electron stochastic thermodynamics, using capacitively coupled single-level quantum dots in the sequential-tunneling regime.** This community provides a direct model for the heat-bath rates, familiar energy-drive and equilibrium-start conventions, and established binary charge sensing. No inspected experiment establishes the complete two-dot preparation, reused control and initial-instrument contract of the present certificate.

The [serial acquisition theorem](FAMILIAR_SWITCH_SERIAL_ACQUISITION.md) retains the **50-million-pair unknown-tilt test** when each recorded-pair law, conditional on the history before the current reset, is within **1 ppm in total variation** of the corresponding law of **one fixed reference model throughout acquisition**. Trials need not be independent. The false-rejection probability is below $`0.047185`$ and nominal-target miss probability below $`0.024949`$. Null and target guarantees use their respective fixed reference models; a new reference cannot be chosen for each word or history.

This operational promise is stronger than control of unconditional marginals or average calibration error. The experimental precedents below do not certify it. The result keeps a fixed final sample count and chooses each setting before its current initial sign; it does not authorize optional stopping or selection using that sign. The reference remains ideal, so a calibrated 1% detector channel is not automatically covered by the 1 ppm allowance.

The ten-source comparison focuses on equilibrium preparation, reused Gibbs energy control and binary endpoint measurement. It complements the [earlier control-scope audit](FAMILIAR_SWITCH_CONTROL_SCOPE_SOURCE_AUDIT.md) and [measurement contract](FAMILIAR_SWITCH_FROZEN_MEASUREMENT.md). Locations identify inspected primary passages; the comparison is not an exhaustive platform survey.

## What the evidence supports for this experiment

The established convention is a population Markov model with a chosen equilibrium ensemble, controlled energy levels, and a fixed kinetic law reused whenever a control value returns. Sources 1–3 make this mathematically explicit. Sources 4–5 show that repeated reference preparation, gate driving and charge recording are experimentally meaningful operations. Sources 6–9 establish substantial measurement and coupled-device ingredients. These are compatible precedents, not a claim that their operating regimes can be combined without additional work.

For the present fixture, the existing charge mapping gives

```math
\mathcal E(n_1,n_2)=\epsilon_1 n_1+\epsilon_2 n_2+U n_1n_2,
\quad S=2n_1-1,\quad Z=1-2n_2,
```

with $`\epsilon_2=-U/2`$, $`\epsilon_1(h)=-U/2-2k_BT h`$ and $`J=U/(4k_BT)`$. These are our algebraic choices within the cited model. Since the current theorem uses $`\tanh J=1/3`$ and $`H=3J`$, its energy scales are $`J=(\log 2)/2`$, $`U=2k_BT\log 2`$ and $`\Delta\epsilon_1=-3k_BT\log 2`$. Older source notes used different operating points; their numerical energy examples should not be imported here.

The term **classical spin** is justified as a binary occupation label in this reduced master equation. It does not require measuring a coherent electron spin. The classical description excludes relevant interdot coherence and unresolved extra levels by assumption. Literal nanomagnets are also observable classical bits, but source 10 uses a different kinetic law. Direct visibility alone does not transfer the current quantitative theorem to magnetic or colloidal switches.

## Continuous monitoring and the first measurement

Continuous charge monitoring is well established by sources 4–7. Extracting only the first and last entries of such a trace is a legitimate way to produce an endpoint record **if the monitored dynamics and the initial instrument obey the specified model**. Keeping the detector on can avoid a separate turn-on pulse, but does not make its influence disappear. Its bias can change transition rates or drive the system away from detailed balance, as source 8 demonstrates.

The ideal initial instrument means that recording `S(x)` leaves the full underlying state `x` unchanged. More generally, the theorem needs a bound on the joint distribution of the recorded initial sign and the full post-readout state. A good marginal bit fidelity does not establish this. Unobserved transitions during finite integration, hidden-charge-dependent errors, and a changed conditional hidden distribution are distinct issues. A continuously monitored stationary process can incorporate its detector into a declared physical model, but then that **monitored** model must satisfy the Gibbs/reversibility/reuse premises.

Thus **continuous binary sensing is a supported convention; exact or sufficiently accurate nondemolition monitoring is an additional instrument condition**. Pulsed sensing is another possible implementation, with its own onset, integration and recovery effects. Neither option is certified by the inspected papers at the tolerances needed to transfer the current sampling theorem.

## What remains a concrete physical promise

| Requirement | Evidence status | Next useful characterization |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One common full-state equilibrium preparation at low field | Standard model input; repeated equilibrium-start operations have direct single-bit precedents. | Characterize the joint two-dot preparation conditionally on past trials. A balanced visible bit alone is insufficient. The nominal target's $`24/\Gamma`$ wait bounds its preparation error from every entering state; it does not prepare every arbitrarily slow rival allowed by the null. |
| One Gibbs energy control and reused `L`/`H` kernels | Directly supported at the sequential-model level. | Compensate cross-capacitance, check barriers and both hidden-charge-conditioned rates at each plateau, and test order/history dependence. A local plunger voltage need not leave tunneling strengths fixed. |
| Binary endpoint observation with a compatible initial instrument | Fast and continuous charge discrimination are demonstrated components. | Establish the joint record/post-readout-state condition, including detector-induced transitions and hidden-charge dependence. No inference from average bit fidelity alone. |
| Physical pulse and dwell clock | A clear separated-timescale convention exists in source 3; real timing corrections appear in source 4. | Characterize finite ramps and detector integration within the whole protocol. A ramp negligible for a selected target is not uniformly negligible for uncapped rival dynamics. |
| Useful acquisition throughput | The serial theorem concerns complete pairs satisfying the 1 ppm conditional contract. | The nominal-target wait plus active evolution costs $`1.28\times10^9/\Gamma`$ for 50 million pairs. Readout, ramps, recovery and calibration add time. The count does not certify null preparation or a complete device throughput. |

The serial result quantifies one sufficient **single-level coupled-dot implementation contract** while leaving its physical validation explicit. The target reset calculation supplies one part of the target-side error budget; the null retains its common stationary preparation and instrument promises. The full conditional bound must cover the combined preparation, readout and controlled evolution errors.

The next scientific question is whether a controlled witness can retain an unknown Gibbs tilt while removing the common-equilibrium preparation promise. This remains a research direction; no preparation-free unknown-tilt theorem is asserted here. The serial theorem retains nominal-target power and does not automatically extend the earlier noisy-channel tests or common-parameter population family to arbitrary different fitted tilts.

## Attribution of the serial concentration bounds

The probabilistic tools are established. The [serial proof](FAMILIAR_SWITCH_SERIAL_ACQUISITION.md) applies them to the registered polynomial score and its certified ranges, conditional variances and Hessian bound.

- **Scalar Freedman inequality:** J. A. Tropp, “Freedman's inequality for matrix martingales,” *Electronic Communications in Probability* **16**, 262–270 (2011), [primary text](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1101.3039), Theorem 1.1, author p. 2. This is the scalar theorem attributed there to Freedman (1975, Theorem 1.6). A conditionally centered martingale with increments bounded above by $`R`$ and predictable quadratic variation bounded by $`\sigma^2`$ has upper-tail bound $`\exp[-a^2/(2(\sigma^2+Ra/3))]`$. The serial certificate applies this scalar result after separating the predictable bias; it does not require a matrix martingale theorem or independent trials. A bound on ordinary unconditional variance alone would be insufficient.
- **Sub-Gaussian quadratic-form inequality:** D. Hsu, S. Kakade and T. Zhang, “A tail inequality for quadratic forms of subgaussian random vectors,” *Electronic Communications in Probability* **17**, no. 52 (2012), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1214/ECP.v17-2079), [published text](https://www.cs.columbia.edu/~djhsu/papers/quadratic-ecp.pdf), Theorem 2.1, Eq. (2.1) and centered Remark 2.2, p. 3. The hypothesis is a joint directional moment-generating-function bound for every direction. A linear change of coordinates gives the anisotropic form used for the Hessian remainder. Independent coordinates and Gaussian observations are not assumed. Iterating conditional directional bounds gives the required global bound for the centered serial sum; its predictable drift is handled separately. Coordinatewise variance estimates alone do not establish that hypothesis.

These inequalities do not prove the physical conditional-TV premise. The new quantitative certificate is their application to this five-setting score under that stated premise; no new general concentration principle is claimed.
