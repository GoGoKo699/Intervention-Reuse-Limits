## Where preparation and numerical tilt disappear

For a singleton sign $`s`$ with positive stationary weight, the actual word-start state is $`x_s`$ whenever that sign occurs. Its true conditional return is $`r_{w,s}=(K_w)_{x_sx_s}`$, independent of preparation. For $`a=1,2`$, set

```math
D_{a,s}=r_{L^aH,s}-r_{L^a,s}r_{H,s},\qquad
B_{a,s}=r_{HL^a,s}-r_{L^a,s}r_{H,s}.
```

The common Gibbs relation gives $`D_{a,s}=\lambda_sB_{a,s}`$ with the same $`\lambda_s=(1+sm)/(1-sm)`$ at both durations. Hence

```math
D_{1,s}B_{2,s}-D_{2,s}B_{1,s}=0.
```

This division-free elimination remains valid when an off-diagonal contribution vanishes. It removes the numerical tilt, while retaining its common Gibbs form. Arbitrary unrelated stationary laws do not provide the same ratio across hidden states.

Zero-weight states require a separate argument. Positive stationary support is closed, so a return path from a zero-weight singleton cannot pass through that support. The zero-weight block has at most two counted states. Cayley–Hamilton for its low-field substochastic block makes the off-diagonal entries of $`L^2`$ proportional to those of $`L`$, giving the same vanishing determinant. No division by zero stationary weights or removal of preparable transient states is used.

## Register the actual active boundaries

Let $`I_t=S(X_t^{\rm in})`$ be the sign after the entire initial acquisition, release, settling and dead time, immediately before the active word. Let $`Y_t=S(X_t^{\rm out})`$ refer to the instant the word ends, before final acquisition. Recorded labels $`A_t,B_t`$ must register these signs.

For an ideal initial detector, $`A_t=I_t`$ suffices. Its earlier operation may change the rival's sign or hidden distribution arbitrarily. Exact sign preservation before word start is unnecessary. Conditional on the full past and actual starting state, however, active evolution must use the prescribed fixed kernels. Persistent apparatus memory that changes those kernels must be counted or excluded; the initial record cannot secretly select another active word.

The finite-error contract bounds

```math
\Pr(A_t\ne I_t\mid\mathcal F_{t-1})\le10^{-6},\qquad
\Pr(B_t\ne Y_t\mid\mathcal F_{t-1})\le10^{-6},
```

with the theorem's joint-history filtration. These joint registration bounds integrate over the current preparation and state. They do not require symmetric channels or errors independent of the postmeasurement state. They are stronger than an unconditional average fidelity over acquisition. The [endpoint-registration proof](FAMILIAR_SWITCH_ENDPOINT_REGISTRATION.md), Sections 1–2, already establishes this boundary convention.

Target preparation remains necessary for power: its **true active-boundary pair laws**, conditional on history, must satisfy the target allowance. A wait before a disturbing initial acquisition alone does not prove that property. The null needs no corresponding closeness condition.

## Rare signs and quotas

A small error before conditioning can become large in a rare observed-sign group. If $`q_s=\Pr(A_t=s\mid\mathcal F_{t-1},w)\gt 0`$, the elementary contamination bound for a singleton return is

```math
\left|\Pr(B_t=s\mid A_t=s,\mathcal F_{t-1},w)-r_{w,s}\right|
\le\min\!\left(1,\frac{p_i+p_f}{q_s}\right).
```

The per-trial registration allowance therefore cannot simply be reused as a conditional-return allowance. Fixed quotas avoid assuming a minimum null sign probability. Group membership uses the initial record and previous counts, before the final outcome. Incomplete required groups at the fixed cap cause nonrejection. A rare or absent sign is not evidence against the null.

For a completed first-$`n`$ group, initial and final error counts give the deterministic comparison

```math
|\widehat r_{w,s}-\widehat r^{\rm oracle}_{w,s}|
\le(E_{w,i}+E_{w,f})/n,
```

with proof-only padding of incomplete true-sign groups. Conditional error-count concentration and predictable selection control the oracle statistic. Target quota completion is a separate power calculation. An average detector error alone does not supply the same exponential count guarantee. The earlier four-word proofs contain this method; the seven-word theorem supplies its own constants.

The physical gain is specifically null-side: neither universal rival equilibration nor preservation of its earlier state is required. Target power, endpoint registration, reused active dynamics and the common force interface remain substantive conditions. The coupled-dot community identified in the [serial audit](FAMILIAR_SWITCH_SERIAL_ACQUISITION_SOURCE_AUDIT.md) remains a credible model anchor, without implying that a device has achieved the combined specification.
