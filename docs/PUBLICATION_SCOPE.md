## What the publication case supports

The case rests on an attainable state-count hierarchy under a declared
control and observation interface, with lower bounds against every
admissible smaller model. The [project narrative](../REVIEW.md) explains
it from the selected Bo–Celani tutorial. Analytic proofs support the
current hierarchy and finite-pulse witness; historical numerical results
support their separately stated tasks.

Broad significance, complete priority and practical device benefit remain
unestablished. Finite ramps, detector behavior, sampling costs, hardware
bits and heat savings have no guarantee for the current three-setting
menu. The restricted menu has no three-state lower bound or fourth-state
necessity result. A substantial interior error gap and the constant-factor
crossover regimes are outside the established order-level claim. These
limits are part of the publication boundary, not new research assignments.
