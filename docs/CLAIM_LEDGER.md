**Updated 24 September 2026.** R61 adds a repeated-readout repair of error-correlated hidden backaction and an integrated five-million-trial calibration gate. Historical rows R1–R60 remain byte-for-byte unchanged. The new design uses 40 million raw readings and retains explicit freshness, protection, stationarity and preparation premises; its complete raw transcript has no established three-state predictor. “Proved” denotes repository derivations with internal checks, not external review or certified priority. Full-suite and separate remote evidence belong to [Verification](VERIFICATION.md).

The preserved heat-bath reference is **R44–R52**: two control orders and initial/final binary observations reveal a conditional covariance that a singleton conformation sector cannot carry. R51 keeps $J=\log3$, lowers $H$ to $\log2$ and shortens both dwells to $5/4$, without adding a preparation, observation or protocol. With target bit errors at most one percent and rival errors in $[0,1/2]$, the stated physical allowances leave recorded joint-TV gap $>0.001$ and general-three-state error at most $2\times10^{-5}$. R52 gives sufficient totals of two million paired trials, or 2.5 million with observed-joint-TV null allowance $10^{-4}$. The first is below R50's necessary count for every test confined to the old $H=\log3$, dwell-$3/2$ design. R49's 32-million/90-million sufficient budgets and R50's calibration-value comparison remain attached to that earlier point. This is a design comparison under the same forms of promises, not a lower bound or optimum for the new point. Capacitively coupled nondegenerate quantum dots give an exact model mapping under specified sequential-tunneling assumptions, and equal attempts are unnecessary for exact prediction; the numerical budgets remain at equal attempts. Preparation and the initial joint instrument remain substantive requirements. No laboratory demonstration, full path-law, generalized-reversal or universal heat conclusion follows.
