**Prepared 23 September 2026.** This claim-to-evidence index supports later drafting. The preceding published baseline is `2923ca8`, with forty mathematical verifiers. R36 gives a general matrix characterization of exact predictor state counts; R37 gives a uniform kinetic-variance compression bound. Their proofs and one new combined verifier have separate internal reviews. “Proved” means a repository derivation with internal checks, not external peer review or certified priority. Full-suite and remote-CI evidence belong to [Verification](VERIFICATION.md).

The simplest organizing results are **R36–R37**. Matched past/future tests permit different positive factors in a stationary predictor; ordinary detailed balance ties them into a Gram. This gives both exact minimum counts for a whole matrix-indexed target class, with a positive matrix-dependent accuracy interval. Kinetic variance controls when hidden memory has little effect on visible means. R22–R26 retain the asymptotic reversal comparison. R27/R31/R34 retain the small finite state gap, and R35/R37 give nine-state and two-state reversible upper bounds at larger errors. These regimes must not be conflated. Ordinary reversal and the shared physical interface remain specified; no universal heat requirement follows.
