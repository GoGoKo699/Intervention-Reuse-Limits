# Four words without a rival preparation promise: source audit

**Focused primary-source audit, 24 September 2026.** The candidate contribution
is a preparation-independent controlled experiment and its particular state-cost
implication. Detailed-balance reciprocity, singleton conditioning, temporal
dimension witnesses and sequential concentration all have close precedents.
The target residual is the repository's existing conditional covariance in a
different observable form; this is not a new covariance mechanism. This bounded
comparison is neither an exhaustive priority search nor external proof review.

## 1. Three hidden states, a visible singleton and equilibrium tests

Christian P. Amann, Tim Schmiedl and Udo Seifert, “Communications: Can one
identify nonequilibrium in a three-state system by analyzing two-state
trajectories?”, *Journal of Chemical Physics* **132**, 041102 (2010).
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.3294567),
[full author preprint](https://arxiv.org/html/1001.0528v1).
Inspected the complete HTML main text, especially Eqs. (1)–(15).

The observation distinguishes state 1 from the compound sector containing
states 2 and 3. Waiting-time laws and the conditional return probability
starting at state 1 determine measurable parameters; an algebraic constraint
then excludes equilibrium for part of the three-state model family. This is
a direct precedent for exploiting a visible singleton and for testing
detailed balance without resolving the other hidden states.

Their experiment uses a sufficiently long binary trajectory under one
time-independent generator. Our four controlled words use two fixed kernels
linked by a specified equilibrium tilt, with arbitrary preparation before each
trial. Rejecting our three-state ordinary class does **not** establish
nonequilibrium: the four-state target is itself reversible at each field.

## 2. General coarse-observation equilibrium criteria

Bingjie Wu and Chen Jia, “Parameter Inference and Nonequilibrium Identification
for Markov Networks Based on Coarse-Grained Observations,” *Physical Review
Letters* **134**, 087103 (2025).
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.134.087103),
[full preprint v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2406.19586v2).
Inspected preprint pp. 1–4, especially Eqs. (1)–(15); the published abstract
was checked, but the published full text was not retrieved. The preprint title
uses “Markovian systems” in place of “Markov Networks.”

The paper derives statistics and necessary equilibrium conditions from an
infinitely long stationary coarse-grained trajectory of a finite Markov
chain. Its three-state reduction explicitly recovers the preceding
Amann–Schmiedl–Seifert criterion. Equilibrium inference under hidden
observation is therefore already a developed subject.

Our proposed task uses four initial/final pair tables and permits
history-dependent null preparation; it does not recover the full generator
or the information in a continuously monitored trajectory. The exact positive
three-state realization and the ordinary four-state lower bound concern
these specified pair tables, not all temporal observations.

## 3. Reciprocal responses and detailed balance

Eugenia Franco, Bernhard Kepka and Juan J. L. Velázquez, “Characterizing the
Detailed Balance Property by Means of Measurements,” *Archive for Rational
Mechanics and Analysis* **250**, article 25 (2026).
[DOI and publisher full text](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00205-026-02183-7),
[publisher PDF](https://link.springer.com/content/pdf/10.1007/s00205-026-02183-7.pdf).
Inspected Sections 2.1 and 3.1, particularly Definition 3.1,
Propositions 3.8–3.9, Lemma 3.11 and Example 3.12; the reciprocity propositions
are on PDF pp. 20–21.

The authors formulate pathwise detailed balance through matrix powers and
reciprocal response measurements. Full detailed balance implies proportional
reciprocal responses; they examine when partial measurements imply stronger
conclusions, including the three-state case and a four-state counterexample.
Thus neither transition reciprocity nor its low-state consequences should
be advertised as new.

Our identity instead compares the two orders of different controlled
kernels, separates the singleton diagonal term, and uses the known tilt to
eliminate the unknown stationary weights. Singleton conditioning makes that
identity independent of the trial's preparation. This combination, followed
by the specified state-cost comparison, is the narrower candidate result.

## 4. Temporal correlations as a memory resource

Costantino Budroni, Gabriel Fagundes and Matthias Kleinmann, “Memory cost of
temporal correlations,” *New Journal of Physics* **21**, 093018 (2019).
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1088/1367-2630/ab3cb4),
[full preprint](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1902.06517).
Inspected Sections II–III.A, preprint pp. 2–5, especially Eqs. (8)–(10).

The finite-state model represents sequential data by fixed input-dependent
output/update maps and an initial distribution. The number of distinguishable
internal states is an explicit resource; an uncounted clock or free changes
of the machine would alter that resource accounting.

Our fixed-kernel requirement and temporal state-count objective follow this
established perspective. The additional structure here is classical
deterministic state-label readout and a shared stationary tilt, with ordinary
detailed balance compared against general stationary implementations.
The proposed advantage is between these two classical model classes, not a
new general memory witness or a quantum advantage.

## 5. Observable constraints that reject a hidden dimension

M. Bruderer et al., “Inverse counting statistics for stochastic and open
quantum systems: the characteristic polynomial approach,” *New Journal of
Physics* **16**, 033030 (2014).
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1088/1367-2630/16/3/033030),
[full preprint](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1311.2673).
Inspected Section 2, pp. 3–5, and Section 3.3, pp. 8–10.

Characteristic-polynomial relations constrain counting cumulants and can
rule out an assumed generator dimension. The setup includes a monitored
transition and long-time counting statistics. A contradiction must be
interpreted together with the assumed stochastic or quantum model class.

This is a direct precedent for obtaining state lower bounds from observable
algebraic constraints without reconstructing every rate. Our data instead
consist of four finite-duration binary snapshot types. The proposed extra
content is the explicit reversible-class exclusion despite arbitrary rival
preparation, together with an exact smaller general realization under the
same specified controls.

## 6. Predictable stratification and sequential Hoeffding bounds

Steven R. Howard, Aaditya Ramdas, Jon McAuliffe and Jasjeet Sekhon,
“Time-uniform Chernoff bounds via nonnegative supermartingales,”
*Probability Surveys* **17**, 257–317 (2020).
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1214/18-PS321),
[full author manuscript](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1808.03204).
Inspected Section 2.4, Lemma 1 and the Ville-inequality argument, and
Lemma 3(c), “Hoeffding I” (pp. 14 and 27 of the retrieved manuscript;
these are not journal page numbers).

Predictably bounded centered increments admit exponential supermartingales
and tail bounds based on accumulated squared ranges. In the proposed test,
stratum eligibility is known after the initial sign and before the final
sign. Inactive increments can be set to zero, and quotas bound the total
range budget. The relevant event is **completion and error**, not an
independence assertion conditional on completing the quotas.

This is standard martingale concentration applied to the experiment's
filtration. Incomplete strata produce nonrejection; target completion and
power require separate bounds. Neither adaptive data retention nor the
particular union-bound accounting constitutes a new concentration theorem.

## 7. Scope of a defensible claim

The candidate is the specific four-word singleton identity, its use without
any rival preparation or mixing promise, and its combination with the
certified three-versus-four state comparison and finite-trial test.
The fixed dynamics, positive stationary laws, shared tilt, faithful true-sign
readouts and sector-preserving initial instrument remain premises. The
target's equilibrium preparation remains part of its power guarantee.

These sources do not supply device feasibility, an electronic-error budget,
or evidence that these premises hold experimentally. The four-word ideal-bit
result and its [new repeated-readout sampling extension](FAMILIAR_SWITCH_PREPARATION_FREE_READOUT.md) have distinct data and instrument scopes. Any manuscript claim should be checked against the precise model and observation task above; manuscript drafting remains deferred while physical specification and the complete prior-art comparison remain open.
