# Current work order: finite-accuracy meaning and novelty

## Starting point

Read the README, core theory, finite-accuracy note, and prior-art audit. The exact two-state-versus-N separation and kernel equations have internal derivations and executable checks. The all-size construction can retain nonvanishing total response with bounded field sensitivity. A reversible surrogate of at most 2q+1 states achieves a protocol-uniform cubic-coefficient error at most U^3 W/q, independent of N.

## Objective

Determine whether these results support a distinctive publishable statement once standard response theory, nonlinear realization, and positive exponential approximation are accounted for. Do not begin a manuscript or large simulation campaign.

## Tasks

1. Audit the exact proof and the paired-state realization against primary realization literature. Reinspect the full texts of the two 2026 leads and the coarse second-order response paper; access failures are not evidence of absence. Record exact theorem correspondence or an explicit remaining distinction.
2. Define the minimax response approximation problem precisely: fixed passive rate k, bounded kinetic sensitivity, bounded protocols, zero-field equilibrium preparation, cubic single-time mean, and analytic autonomous Markov surrogates. Separate known-kernel compression from learning the kernel from data.
3. Seek a nontrivial tolerance-dependent lower bound or a materially sharper upper bound. A finite-sample separating witness for two-state surrogates is a useful first test. Do not assume every nonzero spectral weight is experimentally resolvable, or claim the elementary 1/q upper bound is optimal.
4. Decide whether a finite-field or noisy-observation formulation is necessary for the scientific contribution. If so, derive explicit remainder/identification bounds before adding an application. State which parameters are fixed with system size.

## Deliverable and stopping rule

Deliver a written derivation or a documented reduction to known work, with small reproducible checks. Update the scope and claims if novelty disappears. A negative comparison is a useful result of the audit, not a reason to manufacture additional model assumptions.

Do not change the MIT license, upload the source fiction or conversational material, make acceptance or journal-tier promises, relabel the finite-state model as turbulence, or claim independent validation from internally written tests. Keep public claims synchronized with proofs and verification reports. The repository is public; preserve unrelated owner changes and use non-forced updates.

## Completion criteria

The next checkpoint must identify what has actually been proved, what prior work already supplies, and what precise unresolved statement remains. It must also distinguish exact state count, finite-accuracy state count, kernel-acquisition cost, and finite-field observability. A simulator or a bibliography alone is not completion.
